Research question

How can a news ranker retain the distinction between stated-profile alignment, recorded recommendation context, and prior consumption? PEC-Fuse constructs source-labeled candidate records before scoring, rather than collapsing these signals into one scalar.

MIND provides click histories and logged impressions, not an independent stated profile. The public P proxy and C summaries derive from the same history. Within a query with nonzero mean-history norm, C_mean = ‖mean history‖₂ × p, so the two fields induce the same ordering.

The original slate is retained and ten sampled items are added as weak negatives. E indicates original-slate membership; no original position feature is used. The comparison concerns this expanded pool, not standard logged-slate benchmark performance.

Reading and reproduction

The cached-vector evaluation runner consumes specified BGE vectors and does not regenerate embeddings. Private pools are retrospective; the study does not establish prospective validity of the complete interface.
